# Product Baseline

## Product
Digital Workforce Communication OS.

## Product promise
One company. One workforce identity. One communication system.

## Initial customer
Indian SMEs with approximately 10–250 employees, with an initial Kerala pilot.

## Core capability sequence
1. Tenant and workforce identity
2. Employee directory
3. Presence
4. Messaging
5. App-to-app calling
6. Business voice through authorised provider
7. PBX routing
8. Mobility/eSIM management
9. CRM integration
10. AI communication intelligence

## Non-goals for DWCO 0.1
- own telecom network
- own eSIM core
- PSTN
- call recording
- AI
- Microsoft Teams dependency
- data-centre build
